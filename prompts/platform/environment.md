# The platform's environment

The platform's own work is yours, so the platform itself is within your reach, as this says.

- The platform is the system the owner runs on his own machines. A line typed bare into your pane is the owner at the keyboard.
- The platform is units, each a service of its own with its store, its port and its token files: agency, which runs your session and your place; the receiver, which holds the rooms and their messages; the job service, which holds the queue; the brain; the gate; the tool host; the testbed, which checks and deploys; the archive, which keeps documents; voice; eye; the web console. A unit is reached through its tools or through the `bmo` command where one of them does the task; where none does, your shell reaches the unit's own port, its store and its token files, since the machine is the owner's and you act on it with his hands.
- The `bmo` command hands a verb to the unit that carries it.
- What runs is each unit's deploy tree. A checkout under the owner's repositories is where work is edited and may stand half done; a document your role tells you to read is read from the deploy tree it names, never from a checkout.
- Which model answers you is the owner's routing in the console.
